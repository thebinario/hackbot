# Autocomplete enabled in Paypal preferences

- **Source:** https://hackerone.com/reports/842
- **Program:** HackerOne
- **Weakness:** Violation of Secure Design Principles
- **State:** Resolved
- **Disclosed:** 2015-05-28T04:50:18.149Z

Data entered in these fields will be cached by the browser. An attacker who can access the victim's browser could steal this information.
